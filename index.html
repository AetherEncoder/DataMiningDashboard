    About the data

    Survey. responses were collected through Google Forms from at .

    Cleaning. responses were removed because they listed more than 4 item categories for one purchase. The remaining responses are used everywhere on this page.

    Methods. Association rules were found with Apriori (mlxtend). The spending tier (₱100 and below or above ₱100) was predicted with a Decision Tree, a Random Forest, and Logistic Regression (scikit-learn), tested with stratified 5-fold cross-validation.
